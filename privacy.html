Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Homic collects nothing. The app talks only to the Homic server running on your own Home Assistant, at the address you scanned. The developer runs no server, receives no data, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your devices and your server

  • Your sign-in key and the server's address, in the iPhone's keychain.
  • Your account (name, user name, a hash of your password, which devices are signed in) on your household's Homic server, kept by whoever runs it.
  • What happens in the house (lights, blinds, who pressed what), on that same server, for the Activity page.

The camera is used only to scan a sign-in or pairing code. Local network access is used only to reach your Homic server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the iPhone gives the app a device token from Apple, and the app hands it to your Homic server, which sends reminders through Apple's push notification service. Signing out removes the token from the server.

Away from home

If your household's Homic offers it, the app joins your household's own private Tailscale network with a one-off key your server issues, so it can reach the server away from home. That connection is coordinated by Tailscale Inc. under its privacy policy; its contents are end-to-end encrypted, and the developer of Homic receives nothing.

The demo home

"Try the demo home" shows a sample home built into the app. It sends nothing anywhere.
